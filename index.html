Three steps, then it runs itself

The setup is one afternoon of importing. After that you just open it and answer.

Drop in your old tests

PDF, text, or a paste from anywhere. It pulls out the questions, the choices, the passages, and the answer key — and shows you a preview before saving.

Drill in the gaps

Waiting for a ride, five minutes before class. Pick a subject or a weak topic, answer a handful, close it. Spaced repetition decides what comes back and when.

Watch the gap close

Log your practice-test scores and the app draws the line to your target, in the unit that matters: how many more questions per section you need right.

Built for the way you actually study

Short sessions, honest feedback, and a number that tells you whether it's working.

It tells you what you did wrong

Every miss gets an explanation — from your own material if it came with one, from Claude if you add a key, and always a rule you can carry to the next question. Then it names the habit behind the miss: leaning on NO CHANGE, picking the longest answer, rushing the ones you actually knew.

Your tests, parsed

Handles A–D, F–J, and Math's A–E / F–K. Passages attach to their questions. Paste an answer key in any format and it maps by number.

Spaced repetition that respects your time

Questions you miss come back tomorrow. Ones you've nailed twice come back in a month. Choose smart mix, weak spots only, unseen only, or just what you flagged — then 5, 10, 20 questions, or endless.

Real ACT pacing

Optional per-question clock at section pace — 36s English, 35s Math, 52s Reading and Science — so speed is something you practice, not discover on test day.

Writing prompts too

Pull a prompt, start the 40-minute clock, write. Drafts save as you type.

Your tests stay on your device

Everything — questions, answers, essays, scores — lives in this browser's local storage. There's no server, no account, and nothing is uploaded. Export a JSON backup whenever you want to move it, and delete the whole library in two clicks. The one exception is opt-in and yours to control: if you add an Anthropic API key for written explanations, that question is sent to Claude and nowhere else.
